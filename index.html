About Tebogo

Experience that turns potential into progress.

CareerOS is grounded in Tebogo Masenamela’s journey from early-career logistics and tutoring experience into senior operations leadership and general management.

Real-world leadership. Practical development. A bigger purpose.

Tebogo is a results-oriented supply chain and logistics professional focused on operational transformation, people development, innovation, efficiency and sustainable performance.

His experience spans warehousing, distribution, inventory, transportation and end-to-end logistics solutions, with leadership exposure across FMCG, retail logistics, telecommunications, appliances, shipping, contract logistics and supply chain solutions.

His leadership approach combines strategic thinking, data-driven decision-making, digital transformation, continuous improvement and a strong understanding of human behaviour.

He is passionate about leadership development, systemic improvement, resilient operations and empowering people to perform at their highest potential.

Current leadership General Manager · Nebula Group | Founder · LEAD by ELEVATE
